Questions about flooring interior film

What is KOINTEC flooring interior film?

It is a self-adhesive architectural film, made in Korea, that gives existing surfaces such as cabinets, doors, walls and furniture a flooring finish without replacing them.

How many flooring finishes are in the KOINTEC 27/28 catalogue?
