Questions about special interior film

What is KOINTEC special interior film?

It is a self-adhesive architectural film, made in Korea, that gives existing surfaces such as cabinets, doors, walls and furniture a special finish without replacing them.

How many special finishes are in the KOINTEC 27/28 catalogue?

18 finishes across Glitter, Design Films. Each one is listed on this page with its product code.
