Solid Colour Interior Film

KOINTEC solid interior film covers cabinetry, doors, partitions and furniture in a flat colour, from whites and greys to deep blues, greens and reds. This page lists every solid finish in the KOINTEC 27/28 catalogue with its code: Solid in a matt finish, Solid Glossy for high-shine kitchen and retail fronts, and Embo Solid with an embossed texture. The swatch colours are photographs, so confirm a final colour with a physical sample before a large project.

Questions about solid interior film

What is KOINTEC solid interior film?

It is a self-adhesive architectural film, made in Korea, that gives existing surfaces such as cabinets, doors, walls and furniture a solid finish without replacing them.

How many solid finishes are in the KOINTEC 27/28 catalogue?

65 finishes across Solid, Solid Glossy, Embo Solid. Each one is listed on this page with its product code.
