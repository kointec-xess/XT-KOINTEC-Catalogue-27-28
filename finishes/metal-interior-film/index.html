Questions about metal interior film

What is KOINTEC metal interior film?

It is a self-adhesive architectural film, made in Korea, that gives existing surfaces such as cabinets, doors, walls and furniture a metal finish without replacing them.

How many metal finishes are in the KOINTEC 27/28 catalogue?

16 finishes across Brushed Metal, Mirror. Each one is listed on this page with its product code.
